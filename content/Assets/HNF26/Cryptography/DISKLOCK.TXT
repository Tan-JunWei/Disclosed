 WHAT IS DISKLOCK?
 -----------------
 DISKLOCK keeps your private files safe from nosy coworkers, siblings and
 spies. Locked files are completely unreadable without your password.

 DISKLOCK uses our exclusive XOR-CRYPT(tm) technology. Every single byte of
 your file is combined with your password, over and over, from the first
 byte to the last. Because XOR-CRYPT(tm) does not compress or pad your data,
 a locked file is EXACTLY the same size as the original. No wasted disk
 space!

 USAGE
 -----
     DISKLOCK LOCK   <file>        Locks <file>, creates <name>.LCK
     DISKLOCK UNLOCK <file.LCK>    Unlocks a file

 You will be asked for a password. Passwords must be EXACTLY 8 characters
 long (same as a DOS file name - easy to remember!).

 Every operation is recorded in DISKLOCK.LOG for your convenience.

 IS IT SECURE?
 -------------
 With 8 characters there are over 7,000,000,000,000,000,000 possible
 passwords. Even a fast 25 MHz 386 trying one million passwords per second
 would need more than 200,000 years to try them all!
